Privacy Policy

App: Dari Korean (package: com.darikorean.dari_korean)
Effective date: November 23, 2026
Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dari Korean (“the App”) handles information. Dari Korean is an educational Korean-learning game with an English interface.

1. Summary

The App is designed to work primarily on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not intentionally collect personal identity information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location for advertising or profiling.

2. Information stored on your device

The App may store the following locally on your device:

  • Learning progress (for example, completed chapters)
  • Game scores and best scores
  • Simple app preferences needed to remember your progress

This information stays on your device through local storage (such as app preferences). It is used only to provide the App’s features.

3. Speech / text-to-speech

The App uses on-device text-to-speech features to read Korean and English aloud. Speech is generated for learning playback. The App is not designed to record or upload your microphone audio for our servers.

4. Data we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the App to run third-party advertising networks that track users across apps for ad targeting.

5. Children

The App is a language-learning product and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

6. Google Play and platform services

When you download or update the App through Google Play, Google may process information according to Google’s own policies (for example, download records, crash/device diagnostics if enabled by the platform, and Play integrity systems). That processing is controlled by Google, not by the App’s local learning data store.

7. Data sharing

Because core learning progress is stored locally, we do not operate a Dari Korean account backend that receives your chapter progress. We may share information only if required by law, or as necessary to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the App.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. Uninstalling Dari Korean removes locally stored progress and scores from that device.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate for a local-first mobile app. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, so please also protect access to your device.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
